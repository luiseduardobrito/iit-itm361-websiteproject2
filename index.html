Technology Journey

I am Luis Eduardo Brito, a software engineer and founder based in Sao Paulo. My work sits at the intersection of financial infrastructure, cloud systems, and applied artificial intelligence.

How I Got Here

I started as a freelance web developer, then moved through internships, consulting, and product companies. Over time I shifted from writing individual features to designing platforms that other teams could build on.

That path includes open banking, payment products, and cloud-native services. The through line is the same: take a hard operations problem and turn it into software that people can trust.

What I Care About in Software

  • Reliable systems that stay available under real traffic
  • Clear APIs that other teams can adopt without guesswork
  • Security as a default, not a later patch
  • Small, well-led teams that ship and learn quickly

Current Work with Artificial Intelligence

I am now focused on how AI changes both products and the way engineering teams work. That includes model-backed features, infrastructure for training and inference, and the operating habits needed to keep those systems safe.

  1. Generative tools that help developers write, review, and explain code
  2. AI infrastructure and operations for teams that need more than a demo
  3. Practical uses of machine learning in financial workflows

What I Want to Keep Learning

The next stretch of my work is less about collecting titles and more about depth. I want to understand how to put AI into production systems without losing reliability, privacy, or human judgment.
